# AndoMaps changelog

## 2.18
- New minimap button icon: the compass rose on a full blue face, framed by the minimap ring, instead of a smaller round compass sitting off-centre inside it.

## 2.17
- The world map button sits further in from the map's right edge, clear of the carved border.
- **Shift+drag** the world map button to move it anywhere on the map; it remembers the spot. Shift+right-click puts it back.

## 2.16
- The world map button's shadow is centred, so the compass no longer looks lopsided.

## 2.15
- The world map button is now the AndoMaps compass on its own, with a soft shadow and a glow on hover, instead of a small emblem inside a minimap ring.
- Zeppelin steps show a zeppelin icon.

## 2.14
- Added this changelog. Each release now shows its changes on CurseForge and GitHub.

## 2.13
- First automatic release to CurseForge.

## 2.12
- The minimap button and addon compartment icon are now a simple gold compass.

## 2.11
- New AndoMaps logo and emblem icon on the minimap and world map buttons.

## 2.1
- Engineering transporters: the **Ultrasafe Transporter: Gadgetzan** (Gnomish) and **Dimensional Ripper - Everlook** (Goblin) are used in routes when they're in your bags and off cooldown.
- The Teleports checkbox now also shows for engineers carrying a transporter.

## 2.05
- Zeppelin steps get a zeppelin icon where the client has one.
- `/am icons` shows which flight, boat and zeppelin icons are in use.

## 2.03
- Boat steps show a ship instead of a spyglass.

## 2.02
- The "get this flight path" tip is now a popup: **Go get it**, **Not now** or **Don't ask again**. `/am fptips reset` brings dismissed tips back.

## 2.01
- Flights show a gryphon icon.

## 2.0
- **Flight path tips:** when a flight path you don't have would make a trip much faster, AndoMaps says so and offers to route you there.
- `/am missing` lists the flight paths you still need; the status line shows how many.
- `/am fptips` turns the tips on or off.

## 1.92
- Fixed: routes could never fly to Mount Hyjal or the Riverglades. Their flight paths now use their real names (Summit of Eternity, Tainted Foothills, Farholde Keep, Rog'mar).

## 1.91
- Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace are listed as raids.

## 1.9
- **Raids** has its own list.
- **Nearest bank and auction house:** the Bank and AH buttons, or type `bank` / `ah`.

## 1.8
- Added WoW Forever's new instances: The Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands, City of Dalaran, The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace.

## 1.7
- Mage city teleports and portals are used when they're faster.

## 1.6
- The world map button moved to the top right.

## 1.5
- Click the world map button, then anywhere on the map, to route to that spot.

## 1.4
- The window layers properly: click windows behind it to bring them forward, and click it to bring it back.

## 1.3
- The window can be resized by dragging its bottom-right corner.

## 1.2
- **Auto-fly:** picks the right flight at the flight master. Hold Shift to choose yourself.

## 1.1
- World map button and Alt+click routing; minimap button and addon compartment entry.

## 1.0
- First release: quickest routes by foot, flight path, boat, zeppelin, tram and Hearthstone, with dungeon and raid entrances, an arrow and a step list.
